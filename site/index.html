Ye Booke of Bugs in JAX

as found with Lean

Being a true account of the bugs that dwell in the differentiation rules of JAX, each one tracked down by modelling its lax primitives in Lean 4 and proving what they promise, which they did not always keep.

Reader, be thou warned. These accounts were conjured, not composed. An artificial scribe wrought the model, the proofs and the tellings, proofread by no man. They may be false.

    Proven
    Found, and also proven in Lean. We transcribe JAX's rule from its source into Lean, as written. A theorem then proves that this transcription is wrong, for example that a JVP rule does not compute the derivative, by a concrete counterexample. The proof explains the bug, and carries over to JAX as far as the model is faithful to the source.
